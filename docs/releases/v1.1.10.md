# Niven's Ringworld 1.1.10

## Smoother flight and map views

- Fixed repeated trajectory-prediction exceptions that could cause severe lag near a ringworld.
- Reduced terrain-streaming stalls during fast travel, while keeping terrain detail and coverage through chunk transitions.
- Reduced repeated atmosphere, distant-ring and cloud preparation work. Visual quality, render distances and preset budgets are preserved.
- Added optional aggregate performance diagnostics for troubleshooting; they are off by default.

## Updating

Close KSP, back up your saves, extract into the KSP root, merge GameData and **replace matching files**. Keep your existing config and extension folders. No new save or Ringworld reset is needed; saved layouts, terrain seeds, residents and progress are preserved.

Required mods remain Harmony 2 >=2.2.1.0, ModuleManager >=4.0.0 and one Ringworld config provider. Interstellar configs also need Kopernicus and its dependencies. Clouds 1.0.2, Scattering 1.0.3 and Parallax bridge 1.0.1 are optional companion updates; Cyla remains optional. Standalone ZIPs contain no dependencies.

Parallax Continued requires KSP's **Terrain Detail: High**. Ringworld's own preset can stay Slow or lower. This also resolves the Kopernicus terrain-detail-change warning when it remembers High but KSP starts on Low. The mod does not force that global setting.

## Tested scope

Targeted KSP 1.12.5 Windows/D3D11 tests covered trajectory/streaming, old save-data upgrades, Map/Tracking, clouds, water and Parallax rendering/photo behaviour. Performance gains vary by scene; volumetric clouds remain expensive on the development laptop. The reporter's entire modded craft and every desktop/GPU combination are not certified.
